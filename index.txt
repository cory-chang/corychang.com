|          CORY CHANG          |

Cory Chang is a Strategic Security Consultant at Mandiant, part of
Google, where he advises executive leadership and technical teams
on building threat-informed security programs. He designs targeted
defenses for high-impact threats and leads enterprise-wide
assessments so organizations have a clear strategy that supports
both business and security needs.

Prior to joining Mandiant, he held security roles at CrowdStrike,
Antares Capital, and Kestra Financial. Across those teams, he built
SOC and vulnerability management programs from the ground up and
engineered custom automation tools. He has also guided global
enterprises through large-scale security transformations.

He graduated from the University of Texas at Austin with a B.S. in
Computer Science (Highest Honors) and a minor in Risk Management.
While at UT, he was an Undergraduate Fellow at the Clements Center
for National Security and studied briefly in the School of Security
Studies at King's College London.
