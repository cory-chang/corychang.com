|          CORY CHANG          |

As a Strategic Security Consultant at Google Mandiant, Cory Chang
leverages deep expertise in enterprise security to deliver holistic,
organization-wide assessments that reduce cyber risk and align
security strategy with business objectives. He also excels in
developing targeted programs to address high-impact security risk
areas.

He brings hands-on experience from previous roles at CrowdStrike,
Antares Capital, and Kestra Financial, with a proven track record in
building SOC and vulnerability management programs, developing
custom automation tooling, and guiding global enterprises through
security transformations.

He earned a B.S. in Computer Science with Highest Honors and a minor
in Risk Management from the University of Texas at Austin. While
there, he studied briefly in the School of Security Studies at
King's College London. He was also an Undergraduate Fellow with the
Clements Center for National Security.
