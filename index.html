Cory Chang

As a Strategic Security Consultant at Google Mandiant, Cory Chang leverages deep expertise in enterprise security to deliver holistic, organization-wide assessments that reduce cyber risk and align security strategy with business objectives. He also excels in developing targeted programs to address high-impact security risk areas.

He brings hands-on experience from previous roles at CrowdStrike, Antares Capital, and Kestra Financial, with a proven track record in building SOC and vulnerability management programs, developing custom automation tooling, and guiding global enterprises through security transformations.

He earned a B.S. in Computer Science with Highest Honors and a minor in Risk Management from the University of Texas at Austin. While there, he studied briefly in the School of Security Studies at King's College London. He was also an Undergraduate Fellow with the Clements Center National Security.
